<h1 class="post-title">nullio, a new thing</h1>

<p class="post-meta">
  <strong>{{ author }}</strong> — {{ date.toLocaleDateString('en-US') }}
</p>

<p>
  Out there, the rain was falling. The drops tore through the air and died on the ground. The beginning of winter is always radical. It wasn’t the first time, and somehow, I knew it wouldn’t be the last. My gaze had the look of rainy weather. My heart, impatient, tried to leap from my chest. I clenched my fists. Tried to calm down. Made some coffee.
</p>

<p>
  The moka pot, innocent, guilty of nothing, suffered in my hands. But it — the moka — could still make coffee. The lighter to ignite the flame only worked with gentleness, and in the state I was in, gentleness was a luxury. I searched for another, less sensitive one, with enough gas to ignore rudeness. I lit the fire. Waited for the coffee, listening to the thunder. I didn’t know if it came from outside or from within me.
</p>

<p>
  Idleness wasn’t an option. Being useful was essential. Filling my mind with what mattered was the only way out. I couldn’t listen to the rain — it did me harm. The soundtrack became whatever my playlist sang. Familiar songs are the best: they demand no thought, only presence.
</p>

<p>
  I looked at the door, standing there confronting me with the old doubt between stopping or moving on, love and its difficult things. What your mouth articulated wasn’t inviting. Nor loving. It hurt like a migraine, burned like pepper in the eyes. There are moments when you simply don’t want to be. And all you wish for is another dimension. I felt that way. But I had chosen to be there when I took on that responsibility. As much as indifference, pretense, and pride were hard, that didn’t erase my duty. Not while I was still there.
</p>

<p>
  In my room, there’s a clock. It doesn’t work. It shows 9:57. Between poorly made monologues and the tears falling outside, I found myself staring at it. It was still 9:57. I saw myself like the hands of the clock, trapped in an instant of time I didn’t know how to escape from.
</p>

<p>
  <strong>Outside, the rain was still falling. And in here, it was still 9:57.</strong>
</p>
