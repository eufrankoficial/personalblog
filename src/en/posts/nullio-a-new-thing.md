## Motivation

I decided to create this space as a new way to express a few thoughts and ideas — and maybe share some of what I’ve learned in art and programming.  
I’m also looking for a place outside social media — which, by the way, would make a good topic for another post — where I can exist without having to fit into predefined formats.

I’ve never really taken a blog seriously.  
But this time, I hope to create something that adds a small contribution to the internet — something someone might use, relate to, or simply read and think, *“that’s enough internet for today.”*

## Challenges

Writing in prose is something new to me.  
I usually connect more with rhythm, music, and poetic structure.  
I don’t think I write particularly well — or badly — I just don’t fully master structured writing.  
Still, I feel that writing is a fascinating kind of power, and I’d like to learn how to use it better.  
Maybe this blog will serve as an exercise in that: **shaping thoughts, refining words, finding rhythm.**

Another topic I intend to explore here is knowledge in technology — especially programming, something that’s been part of my life for almost fifteen years.  
So expect some educational, reflective, and curious content about the digital world too.

And, of course, anything related to film, music, and art in general will also find a place here.

#### Welcome

Thank you for being here.  
I don’t expect what I write to change your life, but if these words steal a few minutes away from social media and give you a moment to breathe, that already makes me happy.
